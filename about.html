About Us

Why buy when you can hire the perfect costume? Whether it's for acting, dancing, or singing, we — Tiska Enterprises, situated at Adhartal, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh — treat creating a costume as a work of art that requires ample knowledge, skill, imagination, and proper execution.

We undertake custom costume orders from our clients in accordance with the ideal colour, design, and specific embellishments. We ensure that all costumes are available keeping in mind the generic sizes and measurements of our patrons.

We are one of the leading suppliers of pre-wedding and long-tail gowns on rent, sale, and wholesale, with rentals available Pan India and worldwide shipping on sale orders.

Our Story

Founded in 2012 by Swarit Verma, Tiska Enterprises began as a costume rental shop in Adhartal, Jabalpur, and has since grown into one of the city's most trusted names for gowns, fancy dress costumes, and formalwear on rent, sale, and wholesale.

Whether it's a performer preparing for a stage act, a dancer perfecting a routine, or a couple planning a pre-wedding shoot, our promise has stayed the same: let our clients focus on what they do best, while we take care of the costume — from colour combination and synchronisation for dance ensembles to the fit and finish of every gown.

Our collection today spans pre-wedding and long-tail gowns, maternity and cocktail wear, sarees, convocation robes, fancy dress costumes for kids, mascots, and blazers and suits for men — earning us a loyal, highly-rated customer base across Jabalpur and beyond, with orders now reaching clients Pan India and, for outright purchases, worldwide.
