Perfect! Since you're seeing the Website IP address in Hostinger, we'll use the A record method to point your GoDaddy domain. This keeps DNS management at GoDaddy (good if you have email there).
Step-by-Step: Point Your GoDaddy Domain Using the IP

Copy the IP Address
In Hostinger hPanel, copy the Website IP address shown (it looks like 123.456.78.90).
Go to GoDaddy DNS Settings
Log in to GoDaddy.
Go to My Products → Click Manage next to your domain.
Click DNS (or Manage DNS).

Update the A Records
Find and delete any existing A records for:
@ (this is the root domain)
www (if it exists)

Click Add New Record (or Add):
Type: A
Host/Name: @
Value/Points to: Paste the Hostinger IP address
TTL: Leave as default (or 1 hour)
Save

Add another one:
Type: A
Host/Name: www
Value/Points to: Same Hostinger IP address
Save


Optional but Recommended:
If you want your site to work on both yourdomain.com and www.yourdomain.com, the above covers it.


After Saving

DNS changes can take 30 minutes to 24 hours to propagate (often 1–4 hours).
Check progress here: dnschecker.org — enter your domain and look at A records.
Once it shows the Hostinger IP everywhere, visit your domain in an incognito window.
